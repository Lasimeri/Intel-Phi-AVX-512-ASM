## What the wider set found

The AVX-512 library alone matched on the first run. libc and libstdc++
found two table faults:

- the address size was not matched: `67 E3` is `jecxz`, decoded as
  `jrcxz`; the entries now carry it (byte 15, `EX_ASIZE`);
- the operand-size defaults were unioned per opcode: `senduipi` (`F3 0F
  C7 /6`) forces a 64-bit size, so `rdrand r32` (`0F C7 /6`) came out as
  `rdrand r64`; the defaults are per entry now (`EX_D64`, `EX_F64`).

The remaining differences were vocabulary: iced files `syscall` under
`Call`, `hlt` under `Next`, `ud2` under `Exception`; the check tool
prints iced's words for them.

## The function scan (the same day, later)

`host/asm/phi512/fnscan.S` indexes a function over the decoder: one
entry per instruction with its kind (copied, EVEX, VEX, a branch inside
or out of the function, a direct call inside the object or into the
PLT, an indirect call or jump, a return, a trap), the branch targets
marked, the counts in a record. `tools/fnscan-check.c` runs it over the
